Innovation

EcoShield Purity &
Zero VOC Commitment

We believe that clean air and pristine finishes should go hand-in-hand. By replacing traditional toxic chemicals with organic, low-odour water-based alternatives, Aira Paints safeguards indoor air quality. Our formulations are completely free of lead, mercury, and harsh VOCs.
