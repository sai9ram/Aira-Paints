5-Step Application Guide

1

Surface Preparation

Clean dust, grease, loose paint, and repair plaster cracks using sand grit.

2

Primer Application

Apply one coat of Aira Sealer/Primer to optimize foundation adhesion.

3

First Coat

Apply the first coat of premium paint and allow it to dry for 4-6 hours.

4

Second Coat

Apply the final second coat for rich colour depth and washability strength.

5

Final Inspection

Check for even coverage, brush strokes, and smooth finish under light.
